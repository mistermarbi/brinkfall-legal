Mister Marbi’s LLC is an independent company founded in July 2025 and based in Lincolnwood, Illinois. We build and publish products across a range of different areas — the company is not tied to one industry, and takes on the ideas we think are worth making.

Today that means two mobile applications: a fitness app already in people’s hands, and a strategy game in testing. Both are designed, built and published in-house.

Our products

ModivFit

Fitness · Available now

A fitness app built around competition rather than record-keeping. Users complete challenges, earn points for what they finish, and climb a leaderboard against everyone else doing the same. The aim is to make consistency feel like something worth winning.

modivfit.com

Brinkfall

Strategy game · In testing

A turn-based tactics game for mobile. Units move like chess pieces, but every unit has health, every attack is answered by a counterattack, and the board itself works against you — in one mode the outer ring collapses turn by turn until there is almost nothing left to stand on. Players deploy their own army before the first move, and in multiplayer neither side sees the other’s placements until the match begins.

Currently in closed testing on Android, with releases planned for iOS and desktop.

How we work

Everything is made in-house: design, code, artwork and publishing. Both products are built by a small team rather than commissioned out, which means ideas can be taken on and seen through to release without passing through anyone else’s process.

The company is not a software house by definition. It exists to produce ideas, and software is what those ideas have looked like so far.
