Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Brinkfall does not ask for your name, your email address, or any account sign-up. It has no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

The only information that leaves your device is what is needed to play a multiplayer match: an anonymous identifier that tells the two players apart, and the contents of the match itself.

If you never play multiplayer, nothing leaves your device.

This policy explains what information the Brinkfall mobile game (the “app”) collects, why, and what happens to it.

Brinkfall is published by Mister Marbi’s LLC, an Illinois limited liability company doing business as Mister Marbi (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at brinkfallsupport@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

Anonymous identifier

The first time you use a multiplayer feature, the app creates an anonymous account through Google Firebase Authentication. This produces a random identifier that is not linked to your name, email address, phone number, or Google or Apple account.

It exists for one purpose: to tell the two players in a match apart, so your moves are recorded as yours and your opponent cannot alter your side of the board.

The identifier is stored on your device so that you remain the same player between sessions and can rejoin a match after closing the app.

Match data

While you are playing or waiting for a multiplayer match, the following is stored on our matchmaking service:

  • The settings of the match: board size, game mode, and map
  • A number used to generate the board, so both players see the same board
  • Which units each player brought, and their chosen visual theme
  • Where each player placed their units, and every move made in the match
  • The six-digit match code, and timestamps used to detect abandoned matches

This data describes a game of Brinkfall. It contains nothing about you personally.

Information collected by our service provider

We use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to run multiplayer. In providing that service, Google receives standard technical information including your IP address and basic request details. This is inherent to any internet service and is handled under Google’s own terms, available at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Purchases

If you buy anything in the app, payment is handled entirely by the platform store — Google Play or the Apple App Store — under that store’s own terms. We never see or receive your payment details. The app records only whether an item has been unlocked, and that record is stored on your own device.

What we do not collect

To be explicit, Brinkfall does not collect or use:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information
  • Your precise or approximate location
  • Your contacts, photos, files, camera, or microphone
  • Advertising identifiers — the app shows no advertisements
  • Analytics or behavioural tracking of any kind
  • Crash or diagnostic reporting

The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics software.

Stored only on your device

Your progress is saved locally and is not uploaded. This includes your win and loss record, your settings, your chosen loadout and visual themes, and which items you have unlocked.

Deleting the app removes this local data. Because it is not backed up to us, it cannot be recovered.

How long we keep things

DataRetention
Waiting and in-progress matches Kept while the match is active. Matches that are cancelled or never joined are deleted immediately.
Completed and abandoned matches Removed periodically once finished or inactive.
Anonymous identifier Kept until you delete the app or ask us to remove it.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising or marketing.

The only third parties involved are Google, as provider of the Firebase infrastructure that runs multiplayer and of the Google Play store, and Apple, for purchases made on iOS.

Your opponent in a multiplayer match sees your in-game actions and your chosen visual theme. They do not see your identifier or anything about you personally.

Children

Brinkfall is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it holds nothing that could identify a child. If you believe a child’s information has reached us, contact brinkfallsupport@gmail.com and we will remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to your data, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted.

Because the app holds no information that identifies you personally, the practical form of these rights is deletion. To request removal of your anonymous identifier and any associated match data, email brinkfallsupport@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 30 days. To locate your data we will need enough detail to identify it, such as when you played and the match codes involved, since we hold nothing else that points to you.

You can delete everything stored locally by uninstalling the app.
